# What happens after my product launches?

Launch isn't the end of the engagement unless you want it to be. Most founders roll straight into biweekly sprints to keep shipping improvements, fix what real users surface, and add the next round of features — the same team that built it, not a handoff to a support queue.

If you'd rather take it from here yourself, that's fine too — everything is documented and owned by you, so there's nothing holding the product hostage to us.
